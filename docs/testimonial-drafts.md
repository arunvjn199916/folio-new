# Testimonial drafts — for approval

These quotes are **drafts written for each person to approve or edit**. None of them is on the site yet.
When someone replies "OK" (or sends their own wording), update their entry in `src/content/extras.ts`:
set the quote to the approved text and `approved: true`. The Testimonials section appears automatically
once at least one quote is approved. Optional: add a photo at `media/images/testimonials/<name>.webp`
and set `photo: "testimonials/<name>.webp"`.

> **Client quotes (Aramco, BAT):** only publish with written permission from the company's IR team —
> naming a client implies endorsement and may be covered by an NDA. Without permission, use a neutral
> attribution such as "IR team, Saudi energy company" or leave them out.
